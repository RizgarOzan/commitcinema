Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Reel Studio Publisher is a small app that publishes short videos to one Instagram account we own and sends a link by direct message to people who ask for it in the comments. This page explains what data the app touches.

What we receive

When you comment on one of our posts, Instagram sends the app the comment text, the comment ID and your Instagram user ID. We use them only to check whether you asked for a link and, if so, to send you one private reply.

What we keep

We store the comment ID for up to 7 days so the same comment never gets two messages. We do not store your name, profile, messages or any other personal data, and we do not build profiles of users.

What we share

We do not sell or share your data with anyone. Data is processed only through Meta's Instagram API, by a script that runs on our own computer.

Deleting your data

Deleting your comment removes it from Instagram; our copy of the comment ID expires on its own within 7 days. To ask us to delete anything sooner, email rizgarozan7@gmail.com and we will do it within 30 days.
